## Against the spec and the official samples

Read the VRMC_vrm 1.0 and VRMC_springBone 1.0 specification pages and ran six official models
through the planner: Seed-san, the constraint and twist sample, the two isBinary conformance
models, the MToon UV animation test and Alicia 0.51. All read and plan; no unrecognised
components. Details in `agent/research/vrm-spring-bones.md`.

Four things changed:

- Blink keeps every shape its expression moves, since Basis blinks with the whole array.
  Seed-san's blink was unset before.
- `isBinary` and the three override fields are read, and reported when a choice cannot carry
  them: `vrm.expression.continuous`, `vrm.expression.override`.
- An expression made only of material binds is reported rather than passed over in silence.
- The look-at type is read; `expression` is reported as `vrm.lookAt.expression`.

The decision record for the selector overstated VRM: several expressions may be worn at once
with weights. It now quotes the spec and calls the selector a menu representation.

## Material binds

VRM 1.0 `materialColorBinds` and `textureTransformBinds`, and 0.x `materialValues`, now convert.
The planner indexes every renderer under the root by the names of its materials; the mapper
writes a Vixxy material property on each renderer that uses the material an expression names,
set at that choice and filled from the material as authored at the others, which is the spec's
`base + (target - base) * weight` with weight 1 or 0. Property names come from UniVRM's
`MToon10Properties` table for 1.0 (`_Color`, `_ShadeColor`, `_EmissionColor`, `_MatcapColor`,
`_RimColor`, `_OutlineColor`), and 0.x names the property itself; `_MainTex_ST` carries scale
and offset. The fixture face gained a material and both fixtures a bind.

Seed-san then showed the limit: its emotions scroll a texture on a material that sits on a
renderer with twelve materials, and a Vixxy property block would scroll all twelve. A bind is
written only where the renderer carries that material and nothing else; a shared renderer is
reported as `vrm.expression.materialShared`. Seed-san's binds are therefore reported, not
written; the fixtures, with one material per renderer, convert.

## Why the converted VRM did nothing

Reported again after 0.5.2: the Expression selector changed nothing on the avatar. The controls
were right; something else was writing the face. Read Vixxy's actuation path end to end
(`BakeProperty` resolves the class through `HVR_ComponentDictionary` by `Type.FullName`, the
blendshape by `GetBlendShapeIndex(propertyName)`, and `Apply` calls `SetBlendShapeWeight`), which
all matched what the writer emits. Then UniVRM: `Vrm10Instance.LateUpdate` calls
`Runtime.Process`, whose `MorphTargetBindingMerger.Apply` calls `SetBlendShapeWeight` for every
morph target of every expression, every frame, with the accumulated VRM value, which is zero when
nothing drives it. It also runs its own spring bones and look-at. The converter had left it on
the avatar, so every frame undid the conversion.

Conversion now removes UniVRM's runtime drivers from the converted hierarchy, by type name so
the package stays free of a UniVRM dependency: `Vrm10Instance`, and for 0.x `VRMSpringBone`,
`VRMBlendShapeProxy` and the three look-at components. Data components stay. Reported as
`vrm.runtimeRemoved`, undoable. A test converts the 1.0 fixture and checks the driver is gone
and the joints remain.

Lesson, added to `AGENTS.md`: a plan-level check ("the selector has the right shapes") is not a
runtime check. What else writes the same property every frame has to be asked before a
conversion is called verified.

## The Neutral choice, and a Basis sync

With UniVRM's driver gone the expressions showed, and the eyes looked wrong. The selector's
default choice applied the avatar's own `neutral` expression at full weight: on a VRoid export
that is `Fcl_ALL_Neutral`, a shape VRM applications never apply, since the spec keeps the preset
for backwards compatibility only. Measured on the sample avatar before changing anything (see
the probe numbers below). Neutral is now every shape at zero and the avatar's `neutral`
expression is ignored.

Synced the Basis clone to upstream (five commits). Nothing near the package changed: the
allow-list, jiggle physics, the SDK components and Vixxy are untouched.

## The eyes

With UniVRM gone the eyes tracked but turned too far: the white filled the eye whenever the
head turned. Read Basis's eye driver end to end. `CalibrateOneEye` picks the bone axes closest to
the head's and composes gaze as `initial * offset`, so the VRoid eye bones' 23 degree rest yaw is
handled. The limit is `BasisLocalEyeDriver.maxAngleDeg`, 25 degrees, a driver setting shared by
every avatar; `BasisAvatar` exposes only `EyeLiveliness` and `EyeAttentiveness`, which feed
`BasisEyePersonality` (hold times, jitter, gaze blend) and not amplitude; and the VOR term adds
the head's rotation to the gaze each frame. VRM 1.0 `rangeMap` declares 10 degrees for this
avatar. So the eyes do what Basis does to every avatar; the VRoid eye geometry shows it. Nothing
the converter writes can lower the clamp. The limit is read from the range maps now and reported
as `vrm.lookAt.range`, and the docs name the limitation. A per-avatar eye angle on `BasisAvatar`
would be the upstream fix. The text reader left the `LookAt` block after `OffsetFromHead`, so the
range maps below it were never reached; the fixture test caught it. Shipped as 0.5.7.
