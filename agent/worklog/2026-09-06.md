# 2026-09-06

Asked whether the VRM work follows the standard and was tested beyond one avatar. It had been

## Against the spec and the official samples

Read the VRMC_vrm 1.0 and VRMC_springBone 1.0 specification pages and ran six official models
through the planner: Seed-san, the constraint and twist sample, the two isBinary conformance
models, the MToon UV animation test and Alicia 0.51. All read and plan; no unrecognised
components. Details in `agent/research/vrm-spring-bones.md`.

Four things changed:

- Blink keeps every shape its expression moves, since Basis blinks with the whole array.
  Seed-san's blink was unset before.
- `isBinary` and the three override fields are read, and reported when a choice cannot carry
  them: `vrm.expression.continuous`, `vrm.expression.override`.
- An expression made only of material binds is reported rather than passed over in silence.
- The look-at type is read; `expression` is reported as `vrm.lookAt.expression`.

The decision record for the selector overstated VRM: several expressions may be worn at once
with weights. It now quotes the spec and calls the selector a menu representation.

## Material binds

VRM 1.0 `materialColorBinds` and `textureTransformBinds`, and 0.x `materialValues`, now convert.
The planner indexes every renderer under the root by the names of its materials; the mapper
writes a Vixxy material property on each renderer that uses the material an expression names,
set at that choice and filled from the material as authored at the others, which is the spec's
`base + (target - base) * weight` with weight 1 or 0. Property names come from UniVRM's
`MToon10Properties` table for 1.0 (`_Color`, `_ShadeColor`, `_EmissionColor`, `_MatcapColor`,
`_RimColor`, `_OutlineColor`), and 0.x names the property itself; `_MainTex_ST` carries scale
and offset. The fixture face gained a material and both fixtures a bind.

Seed-san then showed the limit: its emotions scroll a texture on a material that sits on a
renderer with twelve materials, and a Vixxy property block would scroll all twelve. A bind is
written only where the renderer carries that material and nothing else; a shared renderer is
reported as `vrm.expression.materialShared`. Seed-san's binds are therefore reported, not
written; the fixtures, with one material per renderer, convert.
