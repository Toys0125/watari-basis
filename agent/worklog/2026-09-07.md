## Poiyomi URP conversion

- Poiyomi materials are detected by shader identity and by retained serialized `POIYOMI`
  keywords or `ThemeIndex` properties when Unity has reset the shader reference.
- Installed Poiyomi URP shaders are discovered from their render-pipeline tag or URP identity.
  Specialty variants are not chosen as the automatic base target, and there is no URP/Lit
  fallback.
- Editable `.mat` assets under `Assets` are planned as a separate conversion target. Missing
  Poiyomi URP support is reported before conversion with a direct link to the Poiyomi Discord.
- Remapping preserves serialized Poiyomi floats, integers, vectors/colours, textures and UV
  transforms, keywords, render queue, instancing and GI flags. Common BIRP/URP property aliases
  are filled without overwriting an exact preserved target property.
- EditMode tests cover shader/data detection, target selection, plan selection, and material
  migration. Unity execution is pending a remotely reachable immutable commit; this DevSpace
  host has no Unity editor executable.
